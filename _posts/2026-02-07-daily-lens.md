## 📈 Dow Closes Above 50,000 for the First Time

History was made this week: **the Dow Jones Industrial Average closed above 50,000** for the first time ever, capping off what was otherwise a rough week for U.S. equities.

It's one of those milestones that matters psychologically more than fundamentally—round numbers don't change earnings—but it signals just how far we've come since the pandemic lows.

**The context matters though:** this happened during a week where gold is soaring, Bitcoin is tumbling, software stocks are crashing on AI concerns, inflation data is heating up, and consumer confidence is plummeting.

In other words: the Dow hit 50K while everything else was chaos. Classic 2026.

## 🏔️ Milano Cortina Opens the Winter Olympics

The 2026 Winter Olympics officially kicked off Friday in Italy with a slick opening ceremony celebrating the country's history, fashion, and culture. **Sofia Goggia lit the Olympic cauldron** in a moment that had the crowd roaring.

**The subplot:** Political tensions surfaced in crowd reactions—a reminder that the Olympics never exist in a vacuum. VP JD Vance arrived in Milan to represent the U.S., while Snoop Dogg took part in the torch relay (because of course he did).

**The storyline to watch:** Lindsey Vonn is competing *despite a ruptured ACL*. At 41, she's defying every expectation. Win or lose, that's legendary.

Meanwhile, Ukraine has backed calls for a ceasefire during the Games after Italy and Pope Leo urged world leaders to use the Olympics to further peace. Whether Moscow listens is another matter—they launched a *massive* overnight air attack on Ukrainian energy infrastructure Saturday, targeting electricity generation and distribution.

The contrast is stark: ceremonies and sportsmanship in Italy, missiles and blackouts in Ukraine.

## ⚡ United Steelworkers Avert Nationwide Strike

In news that could have tanked energy markets, the **United Steelworkers union adopted a national agreement on pay and benefits Friday**, averting a strike that would have affected 30,000 workers at 26 companies operating oil refineries and petrochemical plants.

**Why it matters:** A strike would have disrupted fuel supply chains across the country. With inflation already running hot and gas prices politically sensitive, this was a quiet but crucial deal.

Energy investors can breathe easier—for now.

## 🇺🇦 Ukraine Peace Talks: Ambitious March Goal, Skeptical Odds

U.S. and Ukrainian negotiators have discussed an **ambitious March target for a Russia-Ukraine peace deal**, according to sources familiar with the talks.

**The reality check:** That timeline is likely to slip because there's no agreement on the key issue—territory. Russia holds ~20% of Ukraine and isn't giving it back voluntarily.

Meanwhile, Russia reminded everyone why they're at the table by launching overnight strikes on Ukraine's power grid. *"Peace talks during bombing runs"* is a vibe.

The Olympics ceasefire proposal is noble, but historically, Putin doesn't do Olympic truces.
