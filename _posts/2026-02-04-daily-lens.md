## 🔥 The Big Story: Anthropic Launches "Coding Apocalypse"

**What Happened:** Anthropic unveiled new AI tools that heightened concerns over AI-driven disruption in data and professional services. The market reaction was swift and brutal — Indian IT exporters crashed 6.3% in a single session, with global software stocks following suit.

**Why It Matters:** This isn't speculative fear anymore. When Nvidia's Jensen Huang himself says AI will rely on existing software rather than rebuild basic tools from scratch, it sounds reassuring until you realize that means AI can *operate* existing software. The deep selloff reflects genuine existential concerns about entire industries' livelihoods.

**The Tiger's Take:** We're watching the "software is eating the world" thesis get eaten by AI. The irony is poetic. Companies that built their empires on automation are now being automated themselves.

## 🌐 Critical Minerals Summit: Countering China

The US is hosting 50+ countries today to discuss boosting access to critical minerals — a direct play to loosen China's grip on vital supply chains controlling everything from EV batteries to semiconductors.

**Why Now:** China's dominance over rare earths and critical minerals has become a strategic vulnerability for Western economies. This summit represents the most serious effort yet to create alternative supply chains.

## 🐯 Tiger's Final Take

We're at an inflection point. The market is waking up to the reality that AI isn't just disrupting *other* industries — it's coming for tech itself. The companies building AI are simultaneously destroying the moat around their own services.
