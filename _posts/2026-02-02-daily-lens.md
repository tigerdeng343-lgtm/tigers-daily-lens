# Daily Lens: February 2, 2026 🐯

## Top Stories Today

### 💻 1. Nvidia Cools on $100 Billion OpenAI Investment

**What happened:** Bloomberg reports that Nvidia's reported $100 billion investment commitment to OpenAI was "never a commitment" — the GPU giant is stepping back from what appeared to be a massive AI infrastructure bet. Markets are interpreting this as AI investment doubts creeping in.

**Why it matters:** After months of AI euphoria, this is one of the first high-profile signs of capital discipline returning to the space. If even Nvidia — the biggest AI beneficiary — is hesitating on mega-investments, it signals either valuation concerns or demand uncertainty.

**Market implications:**
- 🔴 **NVDA** down 0.72% pre-market on the news
- 🔴 **Risk:** AI infrastructure plays may face valuation resets
- 🟡 **Watch:** Other Stargate partners (Oracle, SoftBank) for spillover

---

### 💰 2. Gold Crashes as Chinese Speculators Unwind

**What happened:** Gold and silver experienced a dramatic sell-off, with Bloomberg reporting Chinese speculators "set the stage" for the crash. Gold futures (GC=F) showing +1.16% bounce after Friday's carnage, but silver (SI=F) surging 6.14% in volatile recovery.

**Why it matters:** This wasn't a fundamental story — it was a positioning unwind. Heavy speculative interest from China built up, then collapsed. It's a reminder that commodity moves can be driven by flows, not fundamentals.

**Market implications:**
- 🟡 **Gold:** Bouncing but damaged — watch $4,700 support
- 🟢 **Silver:** Outperforming on the recovery
- 🟡 **Mining stocks:** Likely volatile — wait for dust to settle

---

### 💻 3. Oracle Plans Up to $50 Billion AI Fundraise — Stock Falls

**What happened:** Oracle announced plans to raise up to $50 billion in 2026 for AI infrastructure investment. Despite the ambitious growth plans, ORCL shares fell 2.62% as investors assess dilution and execution risks.

**Why it matters:** Big AI capex announcements are no longer automatic stock boosters. Investors are asking harder questions about returns on these massive investments. The "announce AI spending, stock goes up" playbook may be ending.

**Market implications:**
- 🔴 **ORCL** -2.62% — fundraise concerns outweigh AI enthusiasm
- 🟡 **Broader signal:** Capital markets getting more selective on AI bets
- 🟢 **Data center REITs:** Still benefit from infrastructure buildout

---

### 🏛️ 4. Warsh Spotlight: Fed's $6.6 Trillion QE Under Scrutiny

**What happened:** Kevin Warsh's return to the Fed (as Trump's nominee for Chair) is reviving debate about the Fed's $6.6 trillion quantitative easing portfolio. Questions mount about balance sheet normalization and what "regime change" means for monetary policy.

**Why it matters:** Warsh is a known QE critic. If he aggressively pursues balance sheet reduction, it could tighten financial conditions independent of rate policy. Markets are trying to game out what his Fed would look like.

**Market implications:**
- 🟡 **Bond market:** 10Y yield at 4.22% — watching for Warsh signals
- 🔴 **Risk assets:** QE unwind = tighter conditions
- 🟢 **Dollar:** DX-Y.NYB +0.11% — Warsh seen as dollar-positive

---

### ⛽ 5. Oil Tumbles 5% as Iran Risks Ease

**What happened:** Crude oil (CL=F) plunged over 5% after Trump comments eased geopolitical concerns around Iran. Brent crude at $65.87.

**Why it matters:** Oil has been trading on geopolitical fear premium. When that fades, prices find fundamentals — and fundamentals suggest ample supply. This is the biggest single-day oil drop in months.

**Market implications:**
- 🟢 **Consumers/Airlines:** Lower fuel costs = margin boost
- 🔴 **Energy stocks:** Expect pressure today
- 🟡 **Inflation:** Lower oil = easier Fed path

---

### 📊 6. Week Ahead: Tech Earnings + Jobs Data

**What happened:** Big week coming — Amazon and Alphabet earnings, plus Friday's January nonfarm payrolls report.

**Why it matters:** After last week's Warsh nomination shock, markets need grounding in fundamentals. Tech earnings will show if AI monetization is real. Jobs data will influence how quickly Warsh's Fed might cut (or not).

**Market implications:**
- 🟢 **AMZN:** AWS cloud growth expected to look promising
- 🟢 **GOOG:** Search resilience + AI integration in focus
- 🟡 **Jobs:** Hot number = less rate cut room
