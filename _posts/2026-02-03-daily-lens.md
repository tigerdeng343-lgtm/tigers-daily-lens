## 🚀 Top Story: SpaceX Acquires xAI in Record-Setting Mega-Merger

Elon Musk has merged his two most ambitious ventures—**SpaceX** and **xAI**—creating what's being called the world's most valuable private company. The deal combines a space-and-defense contractor with a fast-growing AI developer, unifying Musk's ambitions in space exploration and artificial intelligence under one roof.

**Why it matters:** This isn't just corporate restructuring. It's the birth of a vertically integrated AI-space powerhouse. Imagine AI systems trained on data from Starlink's global network, optimizing everything from satellite deployment to Mars mission planning. The merger comes ahead of a potential SpaceX IPO, and the combined entity's valuation could be staggering.

**The read:** Musk is betting that AI and space are inseparable futures. With xAI's Grok models and SpaceX's launch dominance, he's positioning to compete with everyone from OpenAI to defense contractors.

## 🇨🇳 Regulatory Watch: China Bans Tesla-Style Hidden Door Handles

In a world-first safety policy, **China has banned hidden car door handles**—the flush designs popularized by Tesla. The regulation requires all EVs sold in China to have externally accessible door handles that first responders can use during emergencies.

**Why it matters:** China is Tesla's second-largest market. This forces a design change for Tesla and other EV makers who've adopted the sleek aesthetic. It's a reminder that China isn't afraid to set its own standards—and force global companies to comply.

**Broader signal:** Expect more safety-focused regulations targeting EV designs. The era of "form over function" in EVs may be ending.

## ⚡ Strategic Play: Trump Launches $12B Critical Minerals Stockpile

The administration is establishing a **strategic reserve for rare earth elements**—the first major U.S. stockpile of critical minerals in decades. The $12 billion initiative aims to reduce dependence on China, which dominates global rare earth production.

**Why it matters:** Rare earths are essential for EVs, wind turbines, defense systems, and electronics. A single supply disruption could cripple U.S. manufacturing. This move signals a serious pivot toward supply chain independence.

**Stocks moving:** Rare earth miners jumped on the news. Watch **MP Materials (MP)**, **Ucore Rare Metals**, and **Energy Fuels (UUUU)**.

### Russia Hits Ukraine Energy in "Most Powerful Blow" of 2026
