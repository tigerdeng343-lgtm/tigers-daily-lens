## 📡 Story #1: Alphabet Drops $91 Billion Bombshell

Google's parent company just committed to capital expenditures of **$91.45 billion** for 2026 — analysts expected $28 billion. Read that again. They're spending *three times* what Wall Street anticipated.

**The thesis:** This isn't just cloud infrastructure. This is Alphabet betting the farm on AI compute, custom chips (TPUs), and the data center arms race. When you're fighting for AI supremacy against Microsoft/OpenAI, Amazon, and a resurgent Meta, half-measures don't cut it.

**Market read:** GOOGL dipped after-hours despite beating revenue estimates. Investors are asking: is this confidence or desperation? My take — when you have Google's cash generation, it's a moat-deepening play. But capex this high means margin pressure for the next 2-3 years.

**Tiger's take:** Long-term bullish, short-term volatile. Watch for follow-through spending announcements from Microsoft and Amazon. This may trigger a capex arms race across Big Tech.

**Watchlist:** GOOGL, MSFT, AMZN, NVDA (the pick-and-shovel play)

## 🚀 Story #2: SpaceX IPO Looms — Starlink's Next Moves Revealed

Reuters reports SpaceX is planning an IPO this year, and Starlink's roadmap leaked:
- **Starlink Phone** — direct satellite connectivity, no ground hardware
- **Direct-to-device internet** — partnering with carriers for dead-zone coverage  
- **Space tracking service** — monitoring satellites and debris

**Why it matters:** SpaceX at IPO would be the most anticipated tech listing since... I don't know, maybe ever? The company runs the world's busiest launch fleet, dominates commercial space, and Starlink is generating real revenue.

**The wildcard:** Elon Musk is Elon Musk. His legal battles (Blade Runner copyright suit, DOGE controversies) and Twitter/X obligations create headline risk. But operationally? SpaceX executes.

**Tiger's take:** No ticker yet, but adjacent plays exist. Watch space ETFs and suppliers. If you can get IPO allocation, it's likely the deal of the decade.

**Watchlist:** RKLB (Rocket Lab), LMT, BA (for comparison), any space SPAC revival

## 🛡️ Story #3: Europe's Defense Awakening

Private credit is flooding into European defense companies. Sweden's Saab raised medium-term growth targets. The Pentagon's drone makers are debuting at Singapore Airshow, eyeing Asia-Pacific expansion.

**The backdrop:** 
- $41 billion U.S. space defense plan targeting Russia/China threats
- Boeing's 777X first production flight in April
- European nations finally spending on defense after years of underinvestment

**The shift:** For decades, ESG-conscious capital avoided defense. Now? Defense IS sustainability — you can't have climate policy without national security. Money is following.

**Tiger's take:** European defense names (Saab, Rheinmetall, BAE Systems) may outperform U.S. primes near-term due to catch-up spending. U.S. names are more mature but still benefit.

**Watchlist:** LMT, RTX, BA, SAAB.ST, RHM.DE, BA.L

## 💻 Story #4: "AI Will Eat Software"

MarketWatch headline of the day: *"Software ate the world. Now Wall Street is worried AI will eat software."*

The software sector is bifurcating into AI winners and AI roadkill. Traditional SaaS companies face existential questions: Does your moat survive when AI can replicate your features?

**Winners:** Companies with proprietary data, network effects, or AI-native architecture
**Losers:** Commodity software that AI can automate away

**Tiger's take:** This is the great sorting. Not all software is equal anymore. Adobe, Salesforce, ServiceNow — each faces different AI exposure. Due diligence just got harder.

**Watchlist:** ADBE, CRM, NOW, PLTR (AI-native winner?)

## 🌍 Story #5: Global Hacking Spree Hits 37 Nations

Bloomberg reports a "vast spying plot" hitting sensitive targets across 37 countries. No attribution yet, but the scale suggests nation-state backing.
