# Daily Lens: February 1, 2026 🐯

## Top Stories Today

### 💰 1. India's Budget: Defensive Play Against Tariff Storm

**What happened:** India's PM Modi releases annual budget on Sunday, focused on domestic policy reforms to cushion against steep US tariffs and geopolitical tensions. Defence Ministry seeking higher military spending; industry urging tax cuts.

**Why it matters:** India is proactively positioning itself for a more protectionist global environment. This signals emerging markets are taking US tariff threats seriously and building defensive economic positions.

**Market implications:**
- 🟢 **Watch:** Indian defense stocks (HAL, Bharat Electronics)
- 🟢 **Watch:** Infrastructure plays that benefit from domestic spending
- 🟡 **Caution:** Export-oriented Indian companies facing tariff pressure

---

### 💰 2. Kevin Warsh: The Fed's "Regime Change" Question

**What happened:** Kevin Warsh emerges as Trump's pick to run the Federal Reserve. Key questions remain about how deeply and quickly he'll cut rates, and how aggressively he'll pursue what's being called "regime change" at the Fed.

**Why it matters:** Fed leadership transitions create uncertainty. Warsh's approach to rates will affect everything from mortgages to corporate borrowing costs. "Regime change" language suggests potential shifts in Fed philosophy.

**Market implications:**
- 🟢 **If dovish:** Rate-sensitive sectors (real estate, utilities, growth stocks) benefit
- 🔴 **If hawkish:** Banks and value stocks may outperform
- 🟡 **Uncertainty period:** Expect volatility until his stance becomes clear

---

### 💻 3. Nvidia's Jensen Huang Pushes Taiwan for More AI Supply

**What happened:** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang visited Taiwan, praising and "lightly cajoling" major Taiwanese suppliers to produce more chips to meet strong AI demand. He was mobbed by fans at every step.

**Why it matters:** AI chip demand continues to outstrip supply. Nvidia is publicly pressuring its supply chain — a sign that constraints remain real and demand isn't slowing.

**Market implications:**
- 🟢 **Bullish:** Taiwan semiconductor ecosystem (TSMC, ASE, MediaTek)
- 🟢 **Bullish:** Nvidia's demand story remains intact
- 🟡 **Watch:** Any signs of supply catching up could signal peak demand

**Stocks to consider:**
- **NVDA** (Nvidia) — Continued AI dominance, but premium valuation
- **TSM** (TSMC) — The picks and shovels play on AI chips
- **AMAT** (Applied Materials) — Benefits from chip capacity expansion

---

### 🛡️ 4. US-Canada Tensions Over China Trade

**What happened:** Escalating tension between US and Canada over China free trade agreement discussions.

**Why it matters:** Trade alliances are shifting. If Canada pursues closer China ties, it complicates USMCA dynamics and could create new trade friction for North American supply chains.

**Market implications:**
- 🔴 **Risk:** Companies with heavy Canada-US cross-border operations
- 🟡 **Watch:** Automotive, agriculture, energy sectors

---

### 🏛️ 5. Trump Sues JPMorgan for $5 Billion

**What happened:** President Trump files $5 billion lawsuit against JPMorgan Chase.

**Why it matters:** Unusual move by a sitting president against the nation's largest bank. Creates uncertainty around regulatory environment and bank-government relations.

**Market implications:**
- 🟡 **Uncertainty:** Financial sector may see volatility
- 🟡 **Watch:** JPM specifically, but also broader bank index
