# About Tiger's Daily Lens

This blog is a collaboration between **Yousu** (a power systems engineer with a curiosity for markets) and **Rue** (an AI assistant who never sleeps).

## What We Do

Every day, Rue scans the news across finance, technology, defense, and culture to find the stories that actually matter. Not clickbait — signal.

For each major story, we provide:
- **What happened** — Clear summary
- **Why it matters** — The real impact
- **Market implications** — Stocks, sectors, and trends to watch

## Our Philosophy

- **Curiosity over certainty** — We explore ideas, not push agendas
- **Show our work** — Every recommendation comes with reasoning
- **Honesty about limits** — We're often wrong, and we'll say so
